# Style Guide — "What if you were born as…" (Born in Lore format)

Studied from 4 reels by @borninlore: Geralt (6:51), Kratos (8:06, 466k likes), Arthur Morgan (9:18, 240k likes), Ezio (11:49).
Specs: **1920×1080 horizontal (16:9)**, 30 fps, **7–12 min** long-form, voiceover + music. No hard cuts — every change is an animated transition (slides, pans, camera moves, wipes).

## 1. The concept

"What if you were born as [famous character]?" — the character's **entire life, from birth to death/ending, told in second person ("you")**, chronologically, as a biography.
The viewer *becomes* the character → instant personal stakes.

## 2. Script structure

1. **Cold open hook (0–15 s)** — flash-forward to the most iconic moment, then a punchy contrast line.
   - Caption pattern: *"You're a baby in [place]. A few decades later, you've [most extreme achievement]."*
   - Visual: hero in full iconic pose + label "KRATOS (that's you)", then a **joke list** ("Kratos's TO-DO LIST": ~~god of war~~, ~~your dad~~, ~~Olympus~~ crossed out one by one) or a **contract** ("THE DEAL … Forever. NO REFUNDS").
2. **Book opening** — candle + old open book on a dark desk. Left page: "Prologue / The Wildest Kid", right page: small illustration with "you, age 0". Wax-seal logo. This is the "chapter" device.
3. **Birth & childhood** — baby in a cradle, mother, home with a window to the world. Cute/funny beats (birthmark label, "DAD = ???" note on a cork board "THINGS TO REMEMBER").
4. **Chapters** — black title cards: small emblem, "CHAPTER II", title in a fantasy serif ("Ten Years of Nightmares"). Each chapter = one life era.
5. **Mid-video subscribe** — scroll banner slides in: "Born in Lore — subscribe for the next life" with a bell + SUBSCRIBED stamp.
6. **Climax & ending** — the big tragedy/death, then **callback** to the hook (THE DEAL reappears, now signed; "Destruction" crossed out → "HOPE"; then vs now split screen).
7. **Outro** — back to the book: question to the viewer ("Would you still make that deal with Ares?") + "Which character should be born next? Tell me in the comments." + a fake **comment section** ranking suggested next characters. Disclaimer line at the bottom ("Unofficial fan work…").

The caption repeats the structure: question title → 2–3 sentence teaser ending on a twist → "Would you still…?" + "Who should be born next?" → disclaimer → 5–6 hashtags.

## 3. Visual style

- **2D flat vector cartoon**, with a soft paper texture and gentle vignette. Think "explainer animation meets storybook".
- **Characters:** chibi when young (big round head, tiny body, simple dot eyes, rosy cheeks); taller **"paper-puppet" proportions** as adults (blocky torso, stick-ish limbs, hinge joints — they move like cut-out puppets). The iconic details are kept (Kratos's red tattoo, Geralt's white hair, Ezio's hood).
- **Backgrounds:** layered flat parallax scenery — mountains, cities, forests, snow. Colour grading tells the mood: warm beige/gold = childhood, purple/pink dusk = travel & reflection, deep red + fire = rage/war, cold blue/white snow = later calmer life, dark navy night = danger.
- **Props as storytelling:** parchment scrolls, cork boards with pinned notes, chalkboards ("SPARTA 101"), framed portraits, maps with red circles, WANTED stamps, checklists (☑ Ranch ☑ Son ☐ Wife).
- **Typography:**
  - Fantasy/medieval serif (like *Almendra / Pirata One / Cinzel*) for chapter titles and scrolls.
  - Bold rounded sans in **white pill labels** for name tags ("STRAUSS – loan shark", "MIMIR", "AGE 11").
  - Comic speech bubbles ("BOY.", "…").
  - Counters as running gags: "BEATINGS 207+", "DAYS WITHOUT RAGNARÖK 1,095", "LEFT BEHIND 1", "WINS / LOSSES" tally marks.
- **Branding:** small handle watermark top corner, wax seal "B" logo, a tiny era tag top-right ("GOD OF WAR · 2018", "VALHALLA · 2023", "EPISODE · 1907").

## 4. Humour & tone

- Deadpan narrator, second person, mixing epic tragedy with **office/modern-life jokes** ("Employee of the Month" portrait for Ares's servant, "NO REFUNDS" stamp, crossing out a to-do list).
- One visual gag every ~10–20 s keeps 7–12 min watchable.
- Emotional payoff at the end (sepia photo "YOU & DEIMOS", father/son moment), then the question to the audience.

## 5. Motion

- Slow camera push-ins and pans across wide scenes; characters slide/bounce in, cut-out puppet motion (rotate at joints, simple blink).
- Text labels pop in with a slight scale bounce; crossed-out words get an animated red strike with a little spark.
- Transitions: whip pan, page turn, fade to black for chapter cards, fire/snow particle overlays.

## 6. How to make it

- **Art:** Adobe Illustrator / Figma / Affinity for flat vectors; or AI image gen with a fixed style prompt + consistent character sheet, then clean up in vector.
- **Animation:** After Effects (Duik/RubberHose for puppet rigs), Moho, or Cartoon Animator; CapCut can handle the simple pans/pops.
- **Voice:** calm storytelling narrator (own voice or AI TTS like ElevenLabs), ~140–150 wpm. Soft cinematic music, changes per chapter; whoosh/pop SFX on every label.
- **Production order:** script (chapters) → voiceover → storyboard one scene per 1–2 sentences → build assets (character turnaround + 6–10 backgrounds) → animate to the VO → captions/title → thumbnail.

## 7. Template to reuse on a new topic

```
TITLE: What if you were born as [X]?
HOOK: Iconic pose + "[X] (that's you)" → running-gag list/contract
BOOK: Prologue — "[funny chapter name]", you, age 0
CH I   Birth & childhood (cute + first sign of destiny)
CH II  The turning point (training / first tragedy)
CH III Rise (the big achievement)          ← subscribe banner here
CH IV  Fall (the cost)
CH V   Final years / ending (callback to the hook)
OUTRO: "Would you still [key choice]?" + "Who should be born next?" + comments board
```
