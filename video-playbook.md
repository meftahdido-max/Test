# "What if you were born as…" — Video Playbook

Built from frame-by-frame study + full transcripts of @borninlore (Kratos 466k likes, Arthur Morgan 240k, Geralt 11k, Ezio 5.5k).

## The numbers
- **Length:** 7–12 min on their channel. Ours: 4–5 min for tests, scale up once a topic proves itself.
- **Pace:** ~150 words/min, short sentences. New visual every **3–5 s**.
- **A joke every 10–15 s.** Dry, one-liners after a big moment: *"Always read the fine print." "Long story." "Not a fashion choice, a promise."*

## Script formula
1. **Hook (0–12 s)** — *"If you were born as X, you'd [A], then [B], then [C]."* A to-do list on screen, each item struck as it's said.
   Then a one-line twist: *"…and it all starts with one really, really bad deal."*
2. **"But right now…"** — rewind icon, pages flip back → *"you're a baby in [place], and [funny detail]."*
3. **Plants** — 3–5 lines like *"Remember that. It matters later." / "Hold that thought." / "Remember that name."*
   Each one gets pinned to the **THINGS TO REMEMBER** board.
4. **Chapters** — 4–6 eras, each with a title card.
5. **Mid-video ask (~40–60% in)** — *"Quick pause. Which character should be born next? Drop a name. The most liked one is next."*
6. **Payoffs** — every plant comes back; circle the note on the board. This is what makes people rewatch and comment.
7. **Ending** — recap the whole life in one sentence of three beats, then *"Not bad for a kid who just wanted X."*
8. **Outro** — the hook's question again: *"So tell me, would you still…?"*

## Visual kit (already built in `tommy/render/`)
- Flat cartoon puppets, paper grain, GTA 6 palette (pink / orange / purple / neon teal).
- Pills for names, counters for running gags (`BEATINGS 207+` style), red strikes, stamps, cork board, parchment lists, chapter cards, book prologue/outro.
- Every label pops with a sound; every scene change gets a whoosh.

## Caption template
```
What if you were born as X?

[2–3 sentence teaser ending on a twist]

Would you still [key choice]? And which character should be born next? Tell me in the comments.

Unofficial fan work. Not affiliated with or endorsed by [publisher].
#Tag1 #Tag2 #Tag3 #gaming #lore
```
